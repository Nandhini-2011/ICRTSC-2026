About Our Institution
SPIHER

SPIHER is a Deemed-to-be University, sponsored by St. Peter's Institute of Higher Education and Research Trust, established in 2008 under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. It was earlier functioning as St. Peter's Engineering College, established in 1993 and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, with eligible programmes accredited by NBA, AICTE, NAAC and ISO. The campus is situated between Annanur and Avadi Railway Stations and is well connected by frequent bus and rail transport.
